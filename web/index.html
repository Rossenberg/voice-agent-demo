Questions

Or just ask Anna — she knows all of this too.

Does treatment hurt?

We use modern local anesthesia, so most procedures are painless. Anesthesia is included in the price.

Do you treat children?

Yes, from age 3, with a dedicated children’s dentist.

Do you take insurance?

We don’t bill insurers directly, but we give you all the documents you need to claim reimbursement.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes — please let us know at least 24 hours in advance. Anna will pass your request to our administrator.

What if I have a dental emergency?

We keep same-day emergency slots on weekdays. For trouble breathing or heavy bleeding, call 911 immediately.

Do you speak English or Russian?

Yes, our team speaks Armenian, Russian and English.

How this demo works

Smile Dental is a fictional clinic. The AI receptionist, the booking system and the dashboard are real and running live.

01 · Voice

Real-time conversation

ElevenLabs Agents handles speech recognition, the LLM, natural voice and interruptions.

02 · Tools

Real booking logic

The agent calls a Cloudflare Worker to check free slots and create bookings. No made-up times.

03 · Dashboard

Every call is logged

Bookings, call outcomes and callback requests appear on the front-desk dashboard instantly.

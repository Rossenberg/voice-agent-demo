# Hero call for the portfolio video (~70 seconds)

One clean call that shows everything: knowledge base → availability check → busy slot → booking → dashboard.
The call audio and transcript are downloaded from ElevenLabs afterwards, so there's no need to screen-record.

**Before you start**
- Quiet room, **headphones on** (no echo), Chrome on the clinic site.
- Speak naturally, short pauses. Wait for Anna to finish (except in step 3).
- Anna's lines will vary a little, which is fine. Just follow the flow.

| # | You say | Anna does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Hi! How much is a professional cleaning?" | Twenty-five thousand drams, about an hour, offers to book |
| 2 | "Great, can I come tomorrow at nine in the morning?" | Checks the schedule. If 9:00 is free she confirms; if not, she offers other times |
| 3 | *(while she lists times, interrupt)* "Sorry — anything around eleven?" | Stops, answers with the nearest time to 11 |
| 4 | "Perfect, let's do that." | Asks for your name |
| 5 | "David Sargsyan." (any made-up name, it shows on the public dashboard) | Asks for the phone number |
| 6 | "Plus three seven four, nine one, two three four, five six seven." | Reads it back |
| 7 | "Yes, that's right." | Repeats service, date, time, name; asks to confirm |
| 8 | "Yes, please book it." | Books, gives booking number, reminds to arrive 10 min early |
| 9 | "Thank you, bye!" | Says goodbye, ends the call |

After the call, tell me "done". I'll pull the recording and transcript and check that the booking reached the dashboard.

> Tip: if something goes wrong, just hang up and do another take. Each take costs about 1 minute of the free plan (15 min/month).

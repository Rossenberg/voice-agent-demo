## Payment

- Cash (AMD), Visa, Mastercard, ArCa, Idram
- **Installments:** 0% for up to 12 months on treatments over 200,000 AMD, through a partner bank (approval required)
- **Insurance:** we don't bill insurance companies directly, but we provide receipts and treatment documents for reimbursement

## Appointment policy

- New patients should arrive 10 minutes early and bring an ID and any previous X-rays.
- Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance. The assistant can't cancel or move a booking itself; it passes the request to the administrator, who calls back.
- If you're running more than 15 minutes late, the appointment may need to be moved.

## Emergencies

- Severe toothache, swelling, a broken tooth, or a knocked-out tooth: we keep **same-day emergency slots** on weekdays. The assistant checks today's availability and passes the case to the administrator.
- Facial swelling with trouble breathing or swallowing, heavy bleeding that won't stop, or a serious injury: **call emergency services at 911 (112 also works) right away.** This is not something to book.

## FAQ

**Does treatment hurt?**
We use modern local anesthesia, so most procedures are painless. Anesthesia is included in the price.

**Do you treat children?**
Yes, from age 3, with a dedicated children's dentist. Book a `kids_visit`.

**How long does whitening last?**
Usually 1–3 years, depending on habits like coffee, tea, and smoking. A cleaning before whitening is recommended.

**Is the first consultation free?**
It costs 5,000 AMD, and it's free if you start treatment the same day.

**Do you do X-rays on-site?**
Yes, panoramic X-rays are done on-site for 8,000 AMD.

**How long does an implant take?**
Usually 3–6 months from placement to the final crown. The exact plan is made at the implant consultation.

**Is the clinic sterile and safe?**
Yes. Instruments are sterilized in an autoclave, and single-use items are used once.

**Can I pay in installments?**
Yes, 0% for up to 12 months on treatments over 200,000 AMD, through a partner bank.

**Do you speak English or Russian?**
Yes, our staff speak Armenian, Russian, and English.

**Do you take insurance?**
We don't bill insurers directly, but we give you all the documents you need to claim reimbursement.
